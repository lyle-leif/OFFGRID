# Tagalog translation review

Every sentence the OFFGRID interface shows, in English and in Tagalog. Please check the Tagalog column: is it natural, clear in an emergency, and does it mean the same as the English?

- Words in `{curly brackets}` are filled in by the app (a name, a distance, a number). Keep them, but move them wherever the Tagalog sentence needs them.
- Guides and hotlines are not here: they stay in English, word for word from the official sources.
- To fix a line, change it in `app/static/lang/tl.json` (same key), then run `.venv\Scripts\python tools\make_translation_review.py` to refresh this page.

459 strings. Generated by `tools/make_translation_review.py`; do not edit by hand.
