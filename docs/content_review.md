# Guide content review

Generated from `data/guides/*.md`. **47 of 59 sections are checked against an official source; 12 are drafts.** The app shows the same status under every section.

## What "checked" means
- The main instructions in the section come from the named official Philippine source, and nothing in the section contradicts it. A few low-risk practical tips may be added. It is **not** a medical or legal endorsement.
- Text is paraphrased, not copied. Hotline numbers are copied exactly.
- Sections with no confirmed official source stay **drafts**, and the app labels them as drafts.

## How the sources were read (be aware)
- **doh.gov.ph and pia.gov.ph block automated readers.** For those pages the wording came from the search engine's copy of the official page. Open the links in a normal browser to confirm.
- **The typhoon sections** were compared with the 2011 *Disaster Preparedness & First Aid Handbook* (Senate Committee on Climate Change with PHIVOLCS, NDRRMC, DOH, PAGASA and others). It is older; the PAGASA wind signals come from PAGASA's 2022 release instead.
- **Hotlines** were copied from each office's own official website on the date shown. Pages that could not be opened (Bauan, Malvar, Tanauan) were left out rather than trusting search summaries.
