**Subject:** Request for the official list of evacuation centers in Batangas, for an offline emergency app

Dear Sir/Madam,

Good day. I am [your full name], a [student / developer] from [your town], Batangas. I am writing to ask for the official list of evacuation centers in the province, and for permission to include it in OFFGRID, a free emergency app I am building for Batangueños.

**About OFFGRID.** OFFGRID works without internet and without electricity from the grid. Each phone keeps an offline copy of a map of all of Batangas, safety guides, emergency hotlines, and directions to the nearest hospitals and evacuation centers. A laptop can act as a local hub so neighbors can share reports such as flooded roads, even when the mobile network is down. The project is open on GitHub: https://github.com/lyle-leif/OFFGRID

**Why I am asking.** The evacuation centers in the app now come from OpenStreetMap, which is community-mapped and not an official list. The app clearly labels them "not official", but residents should be guided to the centers that the province and the municipalities actually designate.

**What I am requesting** (whatever you are able to share):
1. The official list of designated evacuation centers, with the name, barangay and municipality of each.
2. If available: location (address or coordinates), capacity, and which hazards each center is meant for (typhoon, flood, Taal Volcano, tsunami).
3. How often the list is updated, and who I may contact for future updates.
4. Your permission to show this information in the app, credited to the Batangas PDRRMO.

**How the information would be used.**
- It would be shown in the app exactly as provided, with your office named as the source and the date of the list.
- It would be updated whenever you send a newer version.
- The app does not collect names or phone numbers of residents.
- I would be glad to send you a copy of the app or show it to your office, and to follow any conditions you set.

Thank you very much for your time and for your work keeping Batangas safe. I hope to hear from you.

Respectfully,

[Your full name]
[Mobile number] · [Email address]
[Town, Batangas]

## Notes for sending
- **Email first, then follow up.** If there's no reply in about two weeks, call the PDRRMO numbers above or visit the office.
- **If they ask for a formal letter,** print this one, sign it, and bring a short printed description of the app (a screenshot or the README helps).
- **If they share the list,** keep their reply (email or letter): it is your permission. Then OFFGRID can label those centers "Official: Batangas PDRRMO, [date]".
- **Municipal lists** may be easier to get for a single town. The same letter works for a town's MDRRMO.
