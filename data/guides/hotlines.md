## National emergency hotline 911
For any emergency anywhere in the Philippines (police, fire, medical, rescue), call 911. It is the national emergency hotline. Source: DZRH News national hotline list, 7 January 2026.

## Philippine Red Cross national hotline
Philippine Red Cross: call 143, or (02) 8790-2300. National Blood Center: (02) 8527-0000 or (02) 8527-8385 to 95. Source: DZRH News, 7 January 2026.

## Other towns and barangays
For other municipalities in the map area (Bauan, San Pascual, Ibaan, Rosario, San Jose, Cuenca, Alitagtag, Mabini, Tingloy and others), this device has no verified hotline list. Call 911, the Batangas PDRRMO, or your municipal hall. Your barangay hall and its Barangay DRRM Committee are usually the first responders for evacuation. Facility records on the map sometimes include a phone number from OpenStreetMap; treat those as unverified.
