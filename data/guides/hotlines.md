## National emergency hotline 911
<!-- checked: 2026-10-27 | source: Executive Order No. 56; DILG | url: https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/90588 -->
The number to call in an emergency is 911. For any emergency anywhere in the Philippines (police, fire, medical, rescue), call 911. It is the national emergency hotline, free and open 24 hours. It replaced the PNP Patrol 117 line, and since September 2025 the DILG's unified 911 also takes the place of many local emergency numbers.
