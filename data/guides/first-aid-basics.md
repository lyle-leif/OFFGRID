## Before helping anyone
Check that the area is safe for you before approaching an injured person. Call emergency services (911 in the Philippines) for serious injuries. Wear gloves or use a plastic bag as a barrier if available when touching blood.

## Bleeding
Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth or bandage and keep pressing. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top without removing the first layer. Raise the injured arm or leg above heart level if it does not cause more pain. Seek medical help for deep wounds or bleeding that does not stop.

## Cuts and wounds
Wash small cuts with clean water and soap, apply antiseptic, and cover with a clean bandage. Wounds from floodwater, rusty metal, or animal bites have a higher risk of infection and may need a tetanus shot or rabies care; see a health worker.

## Burns
Cool the burn under cool, clean running water for about 20 minutes. Do not use ice, toothpaste, butter, or oil. Remove rings or tight items near the burn before swelling starts. Cover loosely with a clean, non-stick cloth or plastic wrap. Get medical help for large burns, burns on the face, hands, or genitals, or burns on children.

## Possible broken bones
Do not try to straighten the injured part. Keep it still with a splint made from a board, rolled newspaper, or cardboard, padded with cloth and tied above and below the injury. Seek medical care.

## Dehydration and heat exhaustion
Signs include thirst, dry mouth, dark urine, dizziness, weakness, and heavy sweating. Move the person to a cool shaded place, loosen clothing, and give small sips of water or oral rehydration solution. Heat stroke, with confusion, hot skin, or fainting, is an emergency: cool the person quickly with water and fanning and get medical help.

## Unresponsive person
If a person does not respond and is not breathing normally, call 911 and start CPR if you are trained: push hard and fast in the center of the chest, about 100 to 120 times a minute. If the person is breathing, turn them on their side in the recovery position and stay with them until help arrives.
